Sonic has always been a free spirit from his creation. However, as time has gone on, he has served as a source of inspiration for growth in his peers due to his strong sense of self and belief that everyone should have the freedom to choose their own path in life. He would journey to the Starfall Islands with Tails and Amy but he would break out of cyberspace on his own. This ability to travel freely between cyberspace and the tangible world led The End to deceive him into freeing it from its digital prison under the guise that he would only be freeing his friends. Despite constant pushback from Sage and his growing cyber corruption as he helped his friends, he persevered through the pain to follow his own mission. Although he tried to take on the issues of his corruption and The End alone, he had to allow his friends to undo the work he did to free him from his corruption to give them a fighting chance to stop The End.
Hardship is a consistency in everyone’s life, even if it is in varying degrees of difficulty. Despite this, we have to persevere through all problems we face to prevent them from keeping us trapped under the weight of our issues. Some hardships are too difficult to face alone so we have to disregard pride and be open to receiving outside help.
